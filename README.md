# SSW YakShaver Playground

This is the public sandbox for **YakShaver** — the fastest way to report a problem.

Scan a QR code, film what's wrong with your phone, describe it out loud, and hit send. YakShaver watches the video, writes it up, and files a GitHub issue **in this repo** — usually within a minute.

No account. No app to install. Nothing to fill in.

> 🐂 **Every issue here was created by someone pointing a phone at something.** Browse the [Issues](https://github.com/SSWConsulting/SSW.YakShaver.Playground/issues) tab to see what YakShaver makes of a 20-second video.

## Try it now

**[📱 Open the YakShaver QR reporter][qr]** — or scan the QR code on the demo card.

Then:

1. **Tap "Record with camera"** and allow camera + microphone access.
2. **Film the problem** and say what's wrong, and what you expected to happen instead. Talking is the important part — YakShaver listens.
3. **Tap stop**, add your name and a note if you like (both optional), then **Send report**.
4. **Watch it shave.** When it finishes you get a link to the issue it created.

Up to 10 minutes and 500 MB per recording. If you'd rather not film live, **Upload a video** takes one you already have.

### What you get

YakShaver doesn't just attach your video — it watches it and writes the issue:

- A **title** that says what the problem actually is
- A **description** in the team's words, not a transcript
- A **screenshot** pulled from the most relevant frame
- A **link to the video**, so anyone can check the original
- The **`YakShaver`** label, so generated issues are easy to filter

[See an example →](https://github.com/SSWConsulting/SSW.YakShaver.Playground/issues?q=label%3AYakShaver)

## Good to know

⚠️ **This repo is public, and so is everything you send.** The issue, the screenshot and the link to your recording can be seen by anyone. Don't film anything confidential — no customer data, credentials, internal systems or private conversations. Point it at a whiteboard, a demo app, or a genuinely broken teapot.

🧹 **It's a playground.** Issues created here are demo output and get cleaned up periodically. Nothing filed here is tracked or acted on — if you've found a real bug in YakShaver, tell us at [yakshaver.ai](https://yakshaver.ai).

🎙️ **Sound matters more than picture.** YakShaver gets most of its understanding from what you say. A shaky video with a clear explanation beats a steady one in silence.

## Tips for a great report

| Do | Why |
|---|---|
| Say what you expected to happen | Turns "this is broken" into an actionable issue |
| Show the problem, not the whole app | Shorter videos shave faster and read better |
| Mention names and page titles out loud | They end up in the issue, making it searchable |
| Keep it under a minute or two | Long recordings work, but 30 seconds is usually plenty |

## Want this on your own project?

YakShaver is one way in. The full product connects to your GitHub repos or Azure DevOps and turns recordings from anywhere — Teams meetings, OneDrive, the desktop app, a browser extension, or a QR code on a wall — into the perfect PBI.

1. Register at **[yakshaver.ai](https://yakshaver.ai)** and connect your project source.
2. Create your own **YakShaver QR** link and print the code. Stick it wherever problems get found: a test lab, a kiosk, a construction site, a reception desk.
3. Anyone who scans it can report an issue — teammates, clients, visitors — without an account or access to your backlog.

Also available: the **Teams app**, the **browser extension**, the **desktop app**, and an **embeddable widget** for your own site.
